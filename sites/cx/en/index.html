How it works

From feedback to recovery.

The same path for every response, whether it arrives by link or in a spreadsheet.

  1. 01 Ask

    Publish an NPS, CSAT or CES survey and share it by link, QR code or your website.

  2. 02 Collect

    Responses arrive on their own, or you import your spreadsheet with a preview before anything is saved.

  3. 03 Measure

    NPS, CSAT and CES are worked out from the counts, with a notice when the sample is small.

  4. 04 Locate

    Channels, themes and relationships show where the experience gets worse and who it affects.

  5. 05 Follow up

    Any negative response can become a follow-up with an owner and a due date.

  6. 06 Close

    The recovery is recorded, and someone else verifies the close where your organisation requires it.

Built for

Security & privacy

Controls you can show an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus CX works today.

  • Protected sign-in

    Sign-in uses your Pulsus account with a two-step code, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check. Microsoft sign-in too.

  • Protected public surveys

    Cloudflare Turnstile also protects public survey submissions.

  • Each company kept apart

    Row-level security in the database admits only the signed-in company’s rows.

  • Customer data, stricter access

    A named relationship or a customer-level export needs a stricter permission than the aggregate dashboards.

  • Tamper-evident history

    A hash-chained, append-only change history: an altered entry shows.

  • Backed up when it changes

    A change to your company’s data triggers a backup, at most every 15 minutes, with a daily sweep for anything left.

This site and the demo use a fictional organisation only. Your data stays inside the signed-in app.
