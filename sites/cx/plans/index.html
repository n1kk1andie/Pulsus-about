Why Pulsus CX

What they say isn't always what they feel.

Customer feedback arrives through many channels and tends to stay in spreadsheets and email. Without one reading, it is hard to see where the experience gets worse, and whether what you did helped.

01

Scattered feedback

Every channel and location keeps its responses its own way, and nobody sees the whole.

02

Averages that mislead

Averaging location scores hides where the problem is. Pulsus CX builds NPS from the counts.

03

Small samples

A change on a handful of responses looks like a trend. Pulsus CX flags small samples.

04

Unverified follow-up

A complaint "dealt with" is taken as closed. In Pulsus CX, whoever raised it and its owner cannot close it.

How it works

Listening is the start. Closing the loop is the work.

Every piece of feedback follows the same path, from the survey to a verified follow-up. Nothing closes until someone else has checked it.

  1. Set upA CX admin sets up locations, channels and roles.
  2. AskA survey manager builds a survey in Listen and publishes it.
  3. CollectBy link, QR code, website embed, manual entry or a spreadsheet.
  4. MeasurePulse, Channels, Loyalty and Insights update as responses arrive.
  5. Follow upNegative feedback becomes follow-up, with an owner and evidence.
  6. Verify & closeWhoever raised it and its owner cannot close it: someone else does.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

What is Pulsus CX?

Pulsus CX is customer experience and NPS survey software. It measures what your customers think and feel (NPS, CSAT and CES), shows where their experience gets worse, by channel and by location, and tracks whether the follow-up helped.

How much does it cost?

US$149 a month includes your first survey point and 5 users; each extra point, whether a branch or a channel such as the mobile app or contact centre, is US$49 a month, and each extra user US$15. View-only users are free. You can try it free for 14 days, no card. With more than 25 survey points or 50 users, talk to us for an Enterprise quotation.

Do we need integrations?

No. Pulsus CX gives full value from a survey spreadsheet: import an Excel or CSV export of responses, or collect by link, QR code, website embed or manual entry. Duplicates, invalid ratings and responses over 13 months old are flagged, never silently deleted.

How is NPS calculated?

Promoters (9–10) minus detractors (0–6), as a percentage. Enterprise NPS is built from summed counts, never averaged location scores. Small samples are flagged, and a change isn't called significant without enough data in both periods.

Does it use AI?

No. Themes are classified by hand or by rule, and key observations are generated by fixed rules from the reported data. The value of unhappy relationships is the stored value, not a churn prediction.

Who uses it?

Named people with roles: CX admin, CX manager, survey manager, action owner, analyst, executive and auditor. Access can be limited to some locations.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation's data is kept separate. Every change goes into a tamper-evident history, and evidence files are hashed and kept private. Public survey submissions are protected against abuse.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus CX is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

Does it replace our complaints register?

No. Link the case id from your complaints system: Pulsus CX records the follow-up, it doesn't replace the complaints register.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. It is the sister product to Pulsus Accord: Accord asks "did we deliver what we promised?", CX asks "how did the customer feel about it?". Connected to Pulsus Platform, its readings feed your organisation's Operational Health. It also works on its own, in English and Spanish.

Can we try it?

Yes. Try the demo with a fictional organisation's data; it needs only a quick registration. If you already have access, sign in.
