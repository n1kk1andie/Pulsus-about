Evidence, not assumptions

Each letter climbs only as far as the evidence goes.

Communiqué records what it observed and says nothing more. A mail server accepting an email is not the customer receiving it. A letter being posted is not it being delivered. And no letter is ever reported as “read”.

  • Certificates per letter and per batch, each with a verification code
  • SHA-256 fingerprints: check a PDF against its certificate in the browser
  • Public check at /verify shows no customer data

Email evidence ladder

  1. GeneratedThe letter and its fingerprint exist.
  2. ApprovedA checker released it; never its maker.
  3. Submitted to the email providerHanded over for sending.
  4. Accepted by the recipient’s mail serverConfirms sending — not that the person received it.
  5. Letter link viewedSomeone opened the secure link. Not proof of reading.
  6. Bounced or complaintRecorded, and the address is suppressed.

Print & post evidence ladder

  1. GeneratedPrint-ready PDF with an address-window carrier page.
  2. ApprovedReleased by a second person.
  3. Added to the print queueReady for the post room.
  4. PrintedRecorded by the person who printed it.
  5. Posted (dispatch recorded)Confirms posting — not delivery.
  6. Returned (recorded)Returned mail logged against the letter.

Each step lights up only when its evidence exists. A red step is recorded too.
