FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is Pulsus Communiqué?

Pulsus Communiqué is customer correspondence management software. It creates, personalises, approves, issues and tracks customer correspondence at scale. You import a spreadsheet, generate one letter per row from an approved template, have a second person approve the batch, send it by email or print & post, and reconcile every recipient. A certificate records what was sent, how and when.

How much does it cost?

$500 a month, including 5 users, 1,000 letters a month and 3 custom templates. Extra users are $50 each a month; extra letters are $20 for each block of 1,000; extra custom templates are $15 each a month. More than 50 users is Enterprise: talk to us. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card.

What counts as a letter?

Each letter in a file you save — one PDF, one Word document, or one letter inside a zip — counts once, whoever saves it. Each person emailed counts once; resends never count twice. Previews and data checks never count.

What happens when we go over 1,000 letters in a month?

Nothing stops. Additional letters are billed in blocks of 1,000 at $20 a block. You can see this month’s usage against your allowance at any time.

What is a custom template?

The starter letters are free, also after you edit them. Templates you add — blank, made from a pasted letter, or a duplicate — are custom templates. 3 are included; each one beyond that is $15 a month. Prefer not to build it yourself? Ask us with “We’ll build it for you”.

Can we add our team?

Yes. Invite people and set their roles (owner, admin, editor, viewer) on your company page in Pulsus. 5 users are included; each additional user is $50 a month.

Is postage included?

No. Print & post produces print-ready PDFs with address-window carrier pages and records the dispatch your post room carries out. Every letter can be printed and posted with a recorded dispatch trail. Connect your own Lob or PostGrid account in Settings to have Communiqué send approved letters for printing and posting automatically — Stannp and ClickSend connect in Settings with an automatic check — or keep posting them yourself.

Does Communiqué say a letter was received or read?

No. Each letter climbs the evidence ladder only as far as the evidence goes. Accepted by the recipient’s mail server is not received; posted is not delivered; a viewed letter link is not proof of reading. There is no tracking pixel.

How does anyone check a certificate?

Anyone holding a certificate can check it at /verify. The check never shows who the letter was to. SHA-256 fingerprints let you check a PDF against its certificate in the browser.

Is our data secure?

Your library and history are encrypted at rest with a key unique to your company. Batches and campaigns can require a second person, and the server refuses self-approval. Every action goes into the audit trail. The Trust centre has the detail for your security and procurement review.

What happens after the trial?

Choose the plan from your company page on Pulsus. If you don’t, your library stays safe and you can pick up where you left off once a plan is active.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo with a fictional organisation’s data. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own letterhead and a starter letter.
