Pulsus Connect · Enterprise payment orchestration

The payment was approved. So where is it?

From the branch counter to the beneficiary's account, a payment passes through people, systems and institutions. Somewhere along the way, everyone loses sight of it.

Monday · 10:40

Approved.

Inside, the screen says approved. The request was raised, checked and signed off. Then it leaves the building.

Tuesday · 09:12

The customer calls.

"Has my payment gone through?" The branch can see it was approved. Nothing more.

Wednesday · 14:05

The chase begins.

The beneficiary says nothing has arrived. The branch emails Payments. Payments emails the correspondent bank. Everyone waits.

Thursday · 16:30

Nobody can say where it is.

Four days. Five calls. Seven emails. Three teams. One question nobody can answer.

What actually happened

It arrived on Tuesday. Nobody could see it.

The money was credited to the beneficiary at 13:22 on Tuesday, before most of those calls were made. The problem was never the payment. It was visibility.

Enterprise payment orchestration

Raise. Authorise. Route. Track.

Pulsus Connect is one controlled journey for moving money. Requests are raised with their documents, approved under your rules, sent out through the connections you set up, and followed with one reference until they settle.

  • Payment initiationRequests raised at the counter or by your teams, with the signed mandate and documents attached.
  • Multi-level authorisationApproval limits and dual control. Nobody approves their own request, and every decision is on record.
  • ConnectionsConnections to the payment systems you use, such as RTGS, ACH, SWIFT or a wire gateway, and to your core platform. Each is set up and tested with you.
  • RoutingEach payment goes out through the right channel under rules you control. Advanced routing comes with Professional.
  • End-to-end trackingOne reference from initiation to settlement, for your teams and, if you choose, for your customers.

Customers track it themselves

Like tracking a parcel. No phone call.

Your customer gets a text and an email at each step, with their reference and a link. One tap shows where their payment is and when to expect it, on any phone, with nothing to download and no sign-in.

  • A message at every stepReceived, approved and sent, delivered: by text and by email.
  • One link, no app, no passwordThe reference opens the tracking page straight away.
  • A clear answer in plain wordsWhere it is, when it was last updated, when to expect it.
  • Nothing sensitive, everNo amount, account, beneficiary or staff names on the page or in the text.

One reference

Everyone sees the same payment. Only what they should.

Every request carries one reference. The branch, the contact centre and the customer all look it up and get the same answer, each with the detail their role allows.

Illustrative example. The customer's tracking page never receives names, accounts, amounts, the beneficiary or internal notes: that boundary is kept on the server.
