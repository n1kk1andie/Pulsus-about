DELIVERED
01 · At the branch

Raised once, with the mandate.

Branch staff raise the request on one screen and attach the member's signed mandate, as a PDF or a photo. A scanned form can be read straight into the fields, so staff only check and correct. No handwritten form, no second system.

02 · Checked

Verified by someone else.

A verifier opens the mandate beside the request and checks the signature and the details in one view. The person who raised a request can never verify it.

03 · Approved and paid

Authorised by a third person.

A different person authorises it, and the payments team keys it into the right channel (RTGS, ACH or wire) and marks it paid. Pulsus Connect enforces who may do what on the server, not just on the screen.

04 · Delivered

Followed after it leaves your bank.

Most trackers stop at "paid". Pulsus Connect keeps going: in transit, then delivered, as the correspondent advice or settlement confirmation arrives, or through SWIFT gpi where it is connected. The member is sent their payment confirmation.

Where's my payment?

Answered without a phone call.

Every request carries a reference. A member types it into a simple tracking page, like tracking a parcel, and sees where their payment is and when to expect it. Your branch and contact centre see the same answer.

What the tracking page never shows

  • The member's name or account
  • The amount or the beneficiary
  • Bank or payment instructions
  • Internal comments or staff names

That boundary is kept on the server: the page only ever receives the status.
