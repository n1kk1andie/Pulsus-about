FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Pulsus Connect?

Pulsus Connect is enterprise payment orchestration software: it handles payment requests and tracking for banks, credit unions and other organisations that pay out on others' behalf. Branch staff raise a payment request once with the signed mandate attached, three different people raise, verify and authorise it, and the customer can follow the payment to the beneficiary with one reference.

Do we need an integration to use Connect?

No. Every edition works on its own: raise, approve and track payments, import and export them, and keep a full audit trail, then key or upload payments at your bank as you do today. Integrations with your payment networks or core banking are an optional add-on, paid separately.

How are integrations set up?

Self-service wherever possible. Standard integrations that work the same everywhere, such as SWIFT gpi tracking, you switch on yourself in Settings. Integrations that differ for your institution or country, such as your core banking system or your country's RTGS or ACH, are built and tested by Pulsus in a sandbox, then handed over to you: request them in Settings.

Can customers track a payment without an integration?

Yes, up to the point Connect can see. Customers see every step inside your institution: received, verified, approved and sent. After that the payment reads “sent, awaiting confirmation” until your team imports the bank's status file or confirms it. With SWIFT gpi tracking, they follow it all the way to the beneficiary's bank.

When does a payment count?

A payment counts once, when its instruction is authorised in Connect, whether you then export it and key it at your bank or send it through an integration. Payments that are not approved, or are rejected, never count.

What counts as an integration?

A connection is one configured integration with an external system or payment endpoint: a payment connection (RTGS, ACH, SWIFT or an international wire gateway), a core banking connection (your core banking system) or a tracking connection (SWIFT gpi or a bank acknowledgement feed). Microsoft Entra ID sign-in, email, SMS, document reading (OCR) and customer tracking are never counted; they are included.

What is billed, and when?

On Essential and Professional, the annual licence is billed monthly: US$1,250 a month for Essential, US$2,083.33 a month for Professional. Extra users are US$50 (Essential) or US$40 (Professional) per user a month. Banking connections have a one-time setup: a US$12,000 starter pack covers your first 3 (charged when you switch on the first), then US$5,000 each for the 4th to 6th and US$4,500 each from the 7th; each connection is charged once. Each plan also has a minimum monthly payment volume, billed with the licence every month: 500 payments (US$500) on Essential and 1,000 (US$1,000) on Professional. Unused payments expire each month. The connection setup and payments beyond the minimum (US$1 each on Essential, US$0.90 on Professional) are billed after the month, on your next invoice. Third-party service charges are separate.

Can we switch edition?

Yes. A company runs one edition at a time, so moving between Essential and Professional replaces the edition you have rather than adding a second one. Email support@pulsus.tech to arrange it, or sales@pulsus.tech to talk about Enterprise.

Is tracking free for our customers?

Yes. Customers who track their payments never pay and need no account. Customer tracking is never counted as a connection.

What currency are the prices in?

US dollars (USD). Every price on this page is in US dollars.

Can we try it before we buy?

Pulsus Connect has no free trial. Open the live demo instead: the real product with example data, with a narrated tour inside it. Or book a walkthrough with your payments team.

Do we pay per branch?

No. There are no per-branch charges.
