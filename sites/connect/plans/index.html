What counts as a connection

One connection = one system you link to.

A connection is one configured integration with an external system or payment endpoint. There are three kinds.

Payment

RTGS, ACH, SWIFT or an international wire gateway

Core banking

Fiserv Signature or another core banking interface

Tracking

SWIFT gpi or a bank acknowledgement feed

Never counted as a connection. Always included:

  • Microsoft Entra ID sign-in
  • Email
  • SMS
  • Document reading (OCR)
  • Customer tracking

Charges from third-party services are separate. Complex integrations are quoted.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Pulsus Connect?

Pulsus Connect is payment request and tracking software for banks and credit unions. Branch staff raise a payment request once with the signed mandate attached, three different people raise, verify and authorise it, and the customer can follow the payment to the beneficiary with one reference.

What counts as a connection?

A connection is one configured integration with an external system or payment endpoint: a payment connection (RTGS, ACH, SWIFT or an international wire gateway), a core banking connection (Fiserv Signature or another core banking interface) or a tracking connection (SWIFT gpi or a bank acknowledgement feed). Microsoft Entra ID sign-in, email, SMS, document reading (OCR) and customer tracking are never counted; they are included.

What is billed, and when?

On Essential and Professional, the annual licence is billed monthly: US$1,250 a month for Essential, US$2,083.33 a month for Professional. Extra users are US$50 (Essential) or US$40 (Professional) per user a month. The US$12,000 setup for each connection you switch on, charged once per connection, and US$1 per payment processed are billed after the month, on your next invoice. Third-party service charges are separate.

Can we switch edition?

Yes. A company runs one edition at a time, so moving between Essential and Professional replaces the edition you have rather than adding a second one. Email support@pulsus.tech to arrange it, or to talk about Enterprise.

Is tracking free for our customers?

Yes. Customers who track their payments never pay and need no account. Customer tracking is never counted as a connection.

What currency are the prices in?

US dollars (USD). Every price on this page is in US dollars.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days free with no card needed. Sign up with the link for the edition you want: each trial covers only that edition.

Do we pay per branch?

No. There are no per-branch charges.
