What it does

From the teller's count to the treasury position.

The menu follows the day: Pulse, Detail, Performance, Insights, Forecast and Reports, with Settings for administrators.

Pulse

The day's cash position

One headline card for cash in hand, with the reporting status behind it.

  • Cash in hand across branches and ATMs
  • Reporting status: which branches have submitted
  • Branch liquidity outlook for today, tomorrow, 7 and 30 days
  • Today's controls, ATM fleet health and branch ranking

Tills & vaults

The daily sheet

One row per till: the teller and the closing figure, then save.

  • Denomination counter on each row
  • Late entries flagged after the cut-off
  • A branch over its imprest needs signed approval
  • Vault movements and vault position

Reconcile

Reconciliation, three ways

Counted cash against the core banking report and the ledger.

  • Upload the core banking Cash in Hand report (.xlsx or .csv)
  • Variances, missing entries and late entries become exceptions
  • GL Recon: counted cash, the till ledger and the control account
  • Ledger reports are read in your browser

Exceptions

Explained by the branch, closed by a reviewer

Every difference is worked by two people, never one.

  • The branch says what it was and cites the evidence
  • A reviewer accepts and closes it, or sends it back
  • Nobody can close their own explanation
  • Open items age in working days and escalate

Replenish & ATMs

Requests, cash orders and ATM loads

Branches request cash; Treasury approves, schedules and records delivery.

  • Every request carries its pack breakdown
  • Delivery date and ETA set once per cash order
  • ATM days of cash: Watch under 5, Replenishment required under 2
  • Pack order sheet by arrival date

Insights & reports

Forecasts and the reports you file

From “Are we safe?” to the month-end working paper.

  • Liquidity and cash demand forecasts
  • Cash position, movement, exception aging and vault reports
  • Exports to Excel and PDF
  • A full audit trail of who did what, and when

Security & privacy

Controls you can explain to an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus Cash works today.

Protected sign-in

Sign-in uses your Pulsus account, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check, with two-step verification when your account has it.

Your company's data, kept apart

Each request carries its company from a signed session. Headers that claim a company are stripped before any route reads them.

Maker–checker on every difference

A branch explains, a reviewer closes. The server refuses anyone closing their own explanation.

Every step in the audit trail

Entries, changes, approvals and sign-offs carry a name and a timestamp.

Backups when your data changes

A company's data is backed up after it changes, at most every 15 minutes, with a daily sweep for anything still pending.

Ledger files stay with you

GL reports for GL Recon are read in your browser and discarded; only the reconciliation result is kept.
