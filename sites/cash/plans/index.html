How it works

One business day, two pairs of hands.

Every day follows the same path, from the till count to the cash Treasury sends. Nothing closes until someone other than its author has checked it.

  1. Count & fileTellers file their closing counts on the daily sheet before the cut-off.
  2. ReconcileThe counts are run against the core system's Teller Cash Report, three ways.
  3. RaiseEvery variance, missing entry, over-limit position and late entry becomes an exception.
  4. ExplainThe branch picks a category and cites the vault movements already in the app.
  5. Review & closeSomeone else accepts and closes it, or sends it back with a reason.
  6. ReplenishDays of cash say who needs cash first; Treasury approves and schedules it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Cash do?

It brings every till, vault, ATM and cash order across your branches into one live position: what was counted, what the books say, where the two differ, and how many days of cash each location has left. Differences become exceptions that the branch explains and someone else closes, and Treasury plans replenishment from the same figures.

Who is it for?

Banks, credit unions and other institutions that run cash through branches, tellers, vaults and ATMs, and the cash-services clients they supply. It is a financial-institution product; it is not designed for retail tills or petty cash.

How is the price worked out?

$150 a month, plus $10 for each till and $30 for each vault in use. A till marked as a branch vault counts as a vault, not a till. There is no charge per user or per ATM. Prices are in US dollars, after a 14-day free trial with no card.

Do we have to manage vaults in Pulsus Cash?

No. Vault Management is switched on in Settings when you want it. Until then, you are only charged for the tills you use.

Does it replace our core banking system?

No. Pulsus Cash sits beside it. You upload the core system's Teller Cash Report and Pulsus Cash reconciles the day's counts against it, then follows every difference to a close.

Can it handle foreign currency?

Yes. Foreign cash at a till goes on its own line in its own currency, and totals never mix. Insights shows your FX exposure; nothing is converted between currencies.

Who can do what?

Every person has a role: teller, accounting officer, treasury officer, management or admin. Tellers file and explain, accounting officers reconcile and review, treasury officers approve and schedule replenishment, and management reads every dashboard and report without changing anything.

Is our data secure?

Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft, roles limit what each person can see and do, and every step goes into the audit trail.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Cash is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Connect it to Pulsus Platform and every till, vault, ATM and reconciliation feeds your organisation's Operational Health, alongside the other Pulsus applications you run. It also works on its own.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo after a quick registration, with a fictional institution's data. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own branches. For an institution with more than 50 users, talk to us.
