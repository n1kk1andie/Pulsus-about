What's at stake

When a limit is breached, someone will ask who knew.

Good risk management is not only knowing the answer. It is being able to show, at any moment, that you knew, that someone owned it, and what was done.

  • Auditors, inspectors and regulators

    They expect a current view of key risks and a clear record of every finding: who owns it, when it is due, the evidence, and who checked it.

  • The board and its committees

    Members need the same picture every time, judged the same way, not a new deck with new definitions each meeting.

  • Time and exposure

    Every month a finding sits open past its date is a month of exposure. Seeing it early is cheaper than explaining it later.

How it works

Every finding, followed to closure.

Follow-up that owners can complete from their phone in a few taps, and that nobody can close on their own word.

  1. RaiseThe condition, the recommendation, a rating, an owner and a due date.
  2. RespondThe owner’s management response and an action plan with dates.
  3. RemediateThe owner ticks off the plan and adds photos and files from the phone, each stored privately with a fingerprint.
  4. SubmitThe finding goes for validation. Reminders run before, on and after the due date, then escalate.
  5. Validate & closeOnly the person who raised it, or the validator named for it, can validate and close it, or return it. Never the owner.

From target to breach, in plain sight.

Example · card fraud losses · lower is better · target $25k · tolerance $40k · limit $60k

Each indicator has three thresholds you set. Each month’s value is read against them, the same way on every screen and in the committee pack.

  • On targetAt or better than target.
  • Within toleranceOff target, inside tolerance.
  • Outside tolerancePast tolerance, inside the limit.
  • Limit breachPast the limit. Act now.

An example indicator for illustration. In your register, every indicator uses its own thresholds.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Risk do?

It keeps one register for your enterprise risks, findings and key risk indicators. Risks are rated inherent, residual and target against your appetite. Findings from internal audit, external audit, regulators, compliance assessments and your own teams are followed to a validated close. Indicators are judged against their own target, tolerance and limit. A controls library links to the risks, and a committee pack comes out as PDF and Excel.

Does it fit our sector?

Yes. Pulsus Risk is used by banks and financial institutions, and suits government agencies, hospitals and health systems, universities and any organisation with many sites. Pick a sector pack when you set up: the matrix, its words, the sources of findings and the event types follow your sector, and your categories, rating scales and units stay yours to set, from 3×3 to 5×5. The Basel event types are there if you want them.

Who uses it?

Named people with roles: risk admin, risk officer, auditor, owner, contributor, viewer and committee viewer. Access can be limited to the sites, branches or departments a person covers. Owners answer their findings from a phone; the committee reads the position and the pack.

How does follow-up work?

A finding is raised with an owner and a due date. The owner gives a management response and an action plan with dates, then adds evidence, including photos from their phone, and submits. The person who raised it, or the validator named for it, validates and closes it, or returns it. Findings can be reopened or withdrawn. Reminders go before the due date, on it and once it is overdue, then escalate to the owner’s manager and the executive sponsor. They appear in the app and by email.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation’s data is kept separate from every other’s. Evidence files are stored privately, with a fingerprint recorded when each is added. Every change goes into a tamper-evident history. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Risk is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

How do we get our register in?

Add your risks, findings and indicators in the app, or use the Excel import templates for risks, controls, findings and KRI readings, with column mapping. If you used the earlier Pulsus Risk workbook, it can be brought across. KRI readings can also arrive each month through the API.

Will it satisfy our auditors and regulators?

It gives them what they usually ask for: a current view of your key risks against appetite, and a record of every finding with its owner, dates, evidence and who validated it. Pulsus Risk supports your compliance work; it does not certify compliance with any regulation.

Does it cover control testing, obligations and incidents?

Yes. Control testing, risk and control self-assessment, a compliance obligations register, and incidents and loss events are all included, along with policies, risk acceptances, scenarios, third parties and business services.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Pulsus Risk feeds Pulsus Platform’s Risk and Compliance drivers, so a breach shows up in your organisation’s Operational Health beside the other applications you run. It also works on its own.

How much does it cost?

$750 a month, including 5 users; extra users are $75 each a month. Pulsus Risk and Pulsus Quality together, as Pulsus Assurance, are $1,000 a month. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card. For more than 50 users, talk to us.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo with a fictional organisation’s data, after a quick registration. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own data.
