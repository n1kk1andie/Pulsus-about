What it does

From the first rating to the committee pack.

The menu follows the work: Pulse, My work, Findings and Risks, then Assurance, Resilience, Governance and Reports.

Pulse

The risk posture, at a glance

One headline card, coloured by how the month is going.

  • Risks outside appetite, overdue findings and KRIs within tolerance
  • Five measures in the suite's colour bands
  • Findings follow-up over twelve months
  • A residual heat-map you can click into

Findings

Followed to validated closure

Nobody closes a finding on their own word.

  • Management response with an action plan and dates
  • Evidence from a phone, stored privately with its fingerprint
  • Extensions decided by someone else; the original date is kept
  • Closed only when the person who raised it validates it

Risks & appetite

Inherent, residual and target

Every risk rated on your scale and judged against its category's appetite.

  • Basel event types, your own categories, or both
  • Reassessments approved by someone other than the owner
  • The board's appetite statement, cascaded by category
  • Scenario analysis reviewed by the committee

Assurance

Controls, KRIs and self-assessment

Effectiveness is earned from concluded tests, never typed in.

  • Control tests with samples and an independent reviewer
  • KRIs with target, tolerance, limit and direction
  • Self-assessment campaigns answered on a phone
  • Incidents and losses, and risk acceptances with an expiry

Resilience & governance

Suppliers, services, obligations, policies

The registers a regulator asks about, linked to your risks and controls.

  • Third parties with reviews and contract notice dates
  • Important business services with an impact tolerance
  • Compliance obligations attested on a cycle, and regulatory change
  • Policies approved, reviewed and acknowledged by staff

Reports & data

The committee pack, ready when the meeting is

From the spreadsheet you have to the pack the committee reads.

  • Committee pack as PDF and as an Excel workbook
  • Spreadsheet import with a preview before anything is saved
  • A REST API and company keys that feed KRIs automatically
  • Reminders before due, on due and overdue, then escalation

Every device

One account, every screen.

Works in the browser on laptop, tablet and phone — and installs as an app.

  • Laptop The full view, every panel.
  • Tablet For the meeting, the visit or the review.
  • Phone The essentials, wherever you are.

Security & privacy

Controls you can explain to an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus Risk works today.

Protected sign-in

Sign-in uses your Pulsus account or Microsoft, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check, with two-step verification when your account has it.

Your company's data, kept apart

Every table has row-level security forced on, and each request runs pinned to its own company. Tests prove one company can't read another's rows.

Separation of duties

Named roles and access limited to the units a person covers. Reassessments, control tests and findings are signed off by someone else, and the server enforces it.

Tamper-evident history

The change history is append-only and hash-chained per company, so an altered or removed entry shows. Verify it in Settings.

Evidence kept private

Evidence files sit in private storage. Each download goes through an access check, and each file's SHA-256 is recorded.

Backups when your data changes

A company's data is backed up after it changes, at most every 15 minutes, with a daily sweep for anything still pending.
