How it works

One month, from entry to the board.

Every month follows the same path. Each figure has an owner, nothing counts until it is entered or approved, and a locked month stays locked.

  1. Set upUpload your scorecard from the Excel template: your perspectives, objectives, measures, targets and weights.
  2. Assign ownersEach measure belongs to a named person. Units see and enter their own figures.
  3. Enter the monthOwners enter actuals and notes. Figures from your other Pulsus applications wait for review.
  4. ApproveWith approvals on, a figure counts only once someone other than its maker approves it.
  5. Sign off & lockThe month is signed off and locked. A later correction is a recorded restatement.
  6. ReportThe board pack goes out as PDF or PowerPoint, on the schedule you set.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Panorama do?

It is a balanced scorecard management system. It connects strategic objectives, measures, targets and initiatives on a live strategy map, adds the quarter's OKRs, and cascades the scorecard from the board to every department, branch, hospital or campus that has its own.

Can we keep our own perspectives and wording?

Yes. Perspectives come from your own scorecard, in your own words, and Panorama keeps them exactly as written. You set how much each perspective and each measure counts, and the weighted score shows its working. An administrator can move the cut-offs between the six bands, and every change is recorded.

Can we run several companies or units?

Yes. Each company keeps its own scorecard, figures and locked months, strictly separate from every other. Within a company, units such as branches or departments can have their own scorecards that feed the company's, and access can be set by unit so each one sees and enters only its own figures.

How do we get our scorecard in?

Download the Excel template, fill it in, and upload it. You see a preview before anything merges. After that, owners enter each month's figures in the app, and figures from your other Pulsus applications can be linked and approved.

Does it forecast the year end?

Yes. Each measure is projected to the year end with a straight line through its recent months and an 80% range. You can try a different target to ask "what if"; trying one changes nothing that is saved.

What goes to the board?

A branded board pack, as PDF or PowerPoint: cover, pulse, a page per perspective, the measures needing attention with their commentary, the heat-map, the year-end outlook, restated figures and sign-off. It can be emailed to your list on the day you choose.

What is Pulsus Accord, and why is it included?

Pulsus Accord keeps the service levels your departments promise, to each other, to customers and from suppliers, and shows each month whether they were kept. Strategy and service go together, so the $500 a month covers both. Read about Pulsus Accord.

Is our data secure?

Each company's data is kept separate and encrypted with its own keys. Every change goes into a tamper-evident history, and locked months can only be corrected by a recorded restatement. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Panorama is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Spanish. Each person chooses their own, and reports and exports follow it. Amounts carry your company's own currency.

How much does it cost?

$500 a month, which includes Pulsus Panorama and Pulsus Accord together, with no charge per person for up to 50 people. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card. For more than 50 people, talk to us.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo with a fictional company's scorecard, after a quick registration. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own.
