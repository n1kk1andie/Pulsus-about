01 · On the floor

A branch enters its figure.

Harbour branch has its own scorecard. Its customer satisfaction is 86% against 85%: on target. Access is set by unit, so the branch sees and enters its own figures, and no one else's.

02 · The measure

It rolls into the company measure.

Branch measures contribute to the company's customer satisfaction: 84% against 85%. A miss, but inside its 2-point tolerance, so it counts as amber: half. The board gave it a weight of 2.

03 · The objective

The objective takes a score.

Serve customers well rolls up three company measures and two branch measures. Green counts 1, amber ½, red 0, by weight. 83 is in the Watch band.

04 · The perspective

The perspective balances it.

Customer scores 80 across its four measures. A quarterly survey keeps its last result until the next one is in, so it is never counted as a miss in the months between.

05 · The board

One score, and how it was reached.

Four perspectives, weighted 30, 30, 20 and 20, give 91: Healthy. The same figures go into the board pack, as PDF or PowerPoint, on the schedule you set.
