What it does

From the monthly figures to the board's signature.

The menu: Pulse, Details, Strategy, Performance, Insights, Reports and Settings.

Pulse

The one-glance read

The current period, always: no selector, no hunting.

  • Share of measures on target
  • Weighted score by perspective
  • A tile for each perspective
  • The measures that need attention, with their trend

Details

The full scorecard, by month

Grouped and filtered by perspective; tap a measure for its detail.

  • Actual against target, with the trend across periods
  • Commentary, action items and owners
  • Enter the current period's results
  • Earlier months locked; corrections recorded as restatements

Strategy

Objectives, initiatives and OKRs

The strategy behind the numbers, kept with them.

  • Strategy map with cause-and-effect links
  • Initiatives and their milestones
  • The quarter's OKRs
  • Risk-appetite breaches escalated to named contacts

Performance

Every measure, every month

See which perspectives are improving and which keep slipping.

  • Heat map of measure by month
  • On-target rate over time
  • Gaps shown where a month isn't loaded
  • Insights and a year-end outlook for measures at risk

Reports

The board pack, built for you

Each month's pack is drawn from the same figures, never retyped.

  • Narrative and tabular reports, as PDF or Excel
  • Board pack as PDF or PowerPoint
  • Sign-off page with approved figures and restatements
  • A scheduled board pack, sent when due

Your scorecard

Your perspectives, your data

Load your scorecard workbook; Panorama keeps your own perspectives.

  • Uploads previewed, then merged by measure
  • Perspectives named as your company names them
  • Monthly sign-off before a month is locked
  • Scheduled data feeds and webhooks

Security & privacy

Controls you can explain to an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus Panorama works today.

Protected sign-in

Sign-in uses your Pulsus account, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check, with two-step verification when your account has it.

Encrypted with your company's keys

Everything is kept in private storage, encrypted with each company's own versioned keys.

Companies kept strictly apart

A company's session opens that company only; asking for another is refused.

A history that can't be rewritten

Every change records who, when, before and after, in an append-only, hash-chained history.

Locked months stay locked

Unlocking needs a reason, and a correction to a locked month is a recorded restatement.

Backups when your data changes

A company's data is backed up after it changes, encrypted, with regular restore drills.
