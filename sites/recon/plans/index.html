How it works

From raw files to a signed close.

The same six steps for every reconciliation you run, whether it closes daily, weekly or monthly.

  1. CollectStatements, ledger extracts and custodian files, several per side, on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule.
  2. ProveEach file is checked against its own stated total before a row is used. One that doesn't prove is refused.
  3. MatchRows pair on the key columns you choose, and amounts must agree within the tolerance you allow.
  4. ExplainEvery break gets a treatment, an owner and a due date, with evidence attached and its history kept.
  5. ReviewA second person reviews the working paper and approves it. Nobody approves their own work.
  6. CloseThe manager signs off the day; the report goes to Excel or PDF, and open items carry into the next period.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is Pulsus Recon?

Pulsus Recon is bank, ATM and card reconciliation software. It brings in the files from both sides, proves each one against its own totals, matches what agrees and keeps every break in view with an owner and a reason. Bank, ATM cash, securities and any reconciliation you run are closed, reviewed and signed off in one place.

How is the price worked out?

By two things together: the reconciliation processes you run and the people who use it. Processes are priced in graduated bands, like tax bands: the 1st to 5th at $500 each a month, the 6th to 20th at $400 each and the 21st to 50th at $300 each. Five users are included; extra users are $50 each a month.

For example, 8 processes and 7 users: 5 × $500 + 3 × $400 + 2 × $50 = $3,800 a month. More than 50 processes or 50 users is an Enterprise quotation.

What counts as a reconciliation process?

One reconciliation set up in Pulsus Recon, with its own sources, matching and sign-off. An ATM estate reconciled to one general-ledger control is one process; twenty ledger accounts reconciled and approved separately are twenty. A process counts while it is in use: a period saved in the last 35 days. At least one process is charged.

Do people who only view count as users?

No. View-only users are free. The five included users, and any extra users at $50 a month, are the people who prepare, review and sign off.

What files can it read?

SWIFT MT940, MT942 and MT950; ISO 20022 camt.052, camt.053 and camt.054; BAI2; card-scheme settlement reports and card-processor extracts; and your own Excel or CSV layouts, taught once. Several files per side are fine, and a reconciliation can be two-way or three-way.

What happens if a file doesn't add up?

It is refused, with the reason, and nothing from it is matched. An amount that can't be read is flagged, never taken as zero. If a sender changes a layout, Pulsus stops and asks you to teach the new one.

Can someone approve their own reconciliation?

No. The preparer submits; a different person reviews and approves. The manager signs off the day only when every reconciliation due is approved. Every step is in an audit trail, each entry sealed to the one before.

Is our data secure, and where is it stored?

Each company's data is kept apart and encrypted at rest with a key unique to that company. Pulsus Recon is a cloud service; if your regulator or policy requires your data to stay in a particular country, or you want your own hosting, talk to us before you start.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Connect it to Pulsus Platform and its breaks and unexplained breaks feed your organisation's Operational Health, beside the other Pulsus applications you run. It also works on its own.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo after a quick registration, with a fictional group's data. When you are ready, start a 14-day free trial, no card, with your own files. Already have an account? Sign in.
