How it works

One finding's journey.

Follow a single finding from the moment it is seen to the moment it is signed off.

Finding · majorQueue wait over the service standard at the public counter.Owner: Counter supervisorDue in 10 working days
VERIFIED
01 · On site

Seen and recorded.

An inspector checks the counter against the standard on a phone, even without a signal. The shortfall is logged with its severity, a photo and the location.

02 · In review

Owned, with a deadline.

A supervisor reviews it on a tablet, assigns an owner and a due date. The clock starts, and everyone can see it.

03 · In the report

Visible to leadership.

The finding counts towards the defect rate and the trend in the executive report, location by location.

04 · Verified

Fixed, and proven fixed.

The fix is checked and signed off. If the same finding comes back, Quality flags it as recurring.
