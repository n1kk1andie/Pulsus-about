How it works

From the site to a verified fix.

Every inspection follows the same path. Nothing counts until someone else approves it, and nothing closes until someone else has checked it.

  1. 01 Plan

    Programmes of visits set which checklist, which sites, how often or by risk, the window and who goes.

  2. 02 Inspect

    The inspector works through the checklist on a phone. The draft is saved on the device as they go.

  3. 03 Approve

    A supervisor who did not carry it out approves it or sends it back. Only then does it reach the dashboards.

  4. 04 Raise

    A failed check becomes an action with a root cause, an owner, a priority and a due date.

  5. 05 Fix

    The site records what it did and attaches its evidence and photos.

  6. 06 Verify

    Someone who did not raise or own the action checks the fix and closes it, or returns it.

Security & privacy

Controls you can show an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus Quality works today.

  • Protected sign-in

    Sign-in uses your Pulsus account with a two-step code, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check. Staff can also sign in with Microsoft.

  • Each company kept apart

    Row-level security in the database admits only the signed-in company’s rows.

  • Maker and checker

    Nobody approves their own inspection, and whoever raised or owns an action cannot verify it.

  • Tamper-evident history

    Every create and change is logged in a hash-chained history, so an altered entry shows.

  • Backed up when it changes

    A change to your company’s data triggers a backup, at most every 15 minutes, with a daily sweep for anything left.

  • Public pages hold no data

    This site and the demo use a fictional organisation only. Your data stays inside the signed-in app.
