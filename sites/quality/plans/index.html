How it works

Finding the issue is only the beginning.

Every review follows the same path, from the site to a verified fix. Nothing counts until someone else approves it, and nothing closes until someone else has checked it.

  1. ReviewAn inspector checks a sample on a phone. It is saved on the device as they type.
  2. SubmitThe finished review goes to the approval queue.
  3. Approve & publishApproved by someone other than its author, it flows into every dashboard.
  4. Raise an actionWith a root cause, an owner, a due date and, if you like, a named verifier.
  5. Fix & evidenceThe site records what it did and attaches evidence and photos.
  6. Verify & closeSomeone who didn't raise, submit or own it verifies the fix, or sends it back.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is Pulsus Quality?

Pulsus Quality is quality management and defect tracking software. It brings your quality reviews into one place. Inspectors carry out reviews on a phone; supervisors approve them. You see the defect, resolution and recurring rates for every site and process area, an Operational Standard scorecard you set up yourself, and a register of corrective and preventive actions followed through to a verified close.

Does it fit our sector?

Yes. Pulsus Quality is used by financial institutions, and suits government agencies, hospitals and health systems, universities and any organisation with many sites. Your sites, process areas, checks and scorecard are yours to set, and the app's wording follows the sector you choose. Starter packs for each sector are coming.

Who uses it?

Named people with roles: admin, supervisor, inspector, site manager and viewer. Access can be limited to some sites. Inspectors carry out reviews, supervisors approve them and verify fixes, site managers follow their own actions, and executives read the trend and the report.

Can we review on a phone?

Yes. A review is saved on the device as you type, then submitted to the approval queue. Nobody approves their own review. A full offline app, for sites with no signal, is coming.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation's data is kept separate from every other's. Every review and action goes into a tamper-evident history. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Quality is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

Will it satisfy our auditors and inspectors?

It gives them what they usually ask for: evidence that issues were found, owned, fixed and independently checked, with a history of every step. Pulsus Quality supports your compliance work; it does not certify compliance with any regulation.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Pulsus Quality feeds Pulsus Platform's Quality and Compliance drivers, so its readings appear in your organisation's Operational Health beside the other applications you run. It also works on its own.

How much does it cost?

$500 a month, including 5 users; extra users are $50 each a month. Pulsus Quality and Pulsus Risk together, as Pulsus Assurance, are $1,000 a month. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card. For a larger organisation, talk to us.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo with a fictional organisation's data. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own sites.
