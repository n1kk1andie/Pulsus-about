How it works

Enter the month. Accord judges it.

Each standard's owner enters what happened. Accord reads it against the target in force that month, and nothing counts until someone else has approved it.

  • Three ways to enter a result

    Met or not met, "X of Y on time", or a single figure such as an average number of days. Nothing else is asked for.

  • Approved, or returned with a note

    Results go from draft to submitted to approved. An approver can return one for correction, and nobody approves a result they entered or submitted.

  • Missing is never a miss

    A result not entered shows as Missing, then as Submission overdue after the 10th of the next month. Only approved results count towards attainment.

Entering a result on a phone. Demonstration data.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is Pulsus Accord?

Pulsus Accord is SLA and service level tracking software. It keeps the service levels your departments promise, to each other, to customers and from suppliers, written the way staff talk. Each month the owners enter what happened, someone else approves it, and Accord shows what was kept, what broke and what keeps breaking, with a heat-map, insights and three reports.

How much does it cost?

$500 a month, including 5 users; extra users are $50 each a month. Pulsus Accord and Pulsus Panorama together, as Pulsus Panorama + Accord, are $1,100 a month with 5 users, then $100 for each extra user. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card. For volume pricing or your own hosting, talk to us. See Pulsus Panorama's plans.

Do we have to use Pulsus Panorama as well?

No. Pulsus Accord is sold on its own. Use it for your service levels, and add Panorama's balanced scorecard and OKRs when you are ready.

Do we need to track turnaround times or error rates?

No. A month's result is one of three things: met or not met, "X of Y on time", or a single figure. If you don't measure something today, Accord never asks for it.

What happens if nobody enters a result?

It shows as Missing, then as Submission overdue after the 10th of the next month. It is never counted as a miss: only approved results count towards attainment.

Who can enter and approve results?

People hold one or more roles: Accord admin, service owner, contributor, approver and viewer, and access can be limited to some departments. Nobody approves a result they entered or submitted.

Can we bring the spreadsheet we keep today?

Yes. The import wizard reads Excel or CSV and remembers your columns. Or start from your sector's starter accord in quick set-up, keep the standards that fit, set your own targets and assign owners.

Wasn't this called Pulsus Ops?

Yes. Pulsus Accord was formerly Pulsus Ops: the same application, at the same address, with your earlier data brought across.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation's data is kept separate from every other's, and every change goes into a tamper-evident history. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft, and there is no shared password.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Accord is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Pulsus Accord publishes its monthly reading to Pulsus Platform, so your service levels appear in your organisation's Operational Health beside the other applications you run. It also works on its own.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Try the live demo after a quick registration, with a fictional organisation's data. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own service levels. Already a customer? Sign in.
