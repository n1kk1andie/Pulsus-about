What it does

From the promise to the monthly report.

The menu is short: Pulse, Commitments, Performance, Insights and Reports, with Settings for administrators.

Pulse

The month's attainment

One headline card, coloured by the attainment band.

  • Standards met, and how many have reported
  • Department scores in the six Pulsus bands
  • A short list of what needs attention
  • Key observations from the month's figures

Commitments

Standards written the way staff talk

“Complaints answered within 5 working days”, not a code.

  • Services, each holding several standards
  • An owner, a provider and a recipient for each
  • Targets that change from a date, keeping history
  • Starter templates by sector

Performance

Results entered, then approved

Owners enter each month's result; someone else approves it.

  • Met or not met, “X of Y on time”, or a single figure
  • Draft, submitted, approved
  • Returned for correction with a note
  • Nobody approves a result they entered or submitted

Insights

Where service slips, and how often

The patterns behind the month's number.

  • Attainment over the last twelve months
  • Recurring shortfalls
  • Best and worst months
  • Departments compared

Reports

A monthly pack, ready to send

PDF for sharing, Excel for working.

  • The monthly service-level report
  • The standards register
  • The recurring-shortfall report
  • Provisional results are marked as such

Set-up & alerts

Running in an afternoon

Pick a sector, add departments and tick the service levels you want.

  • Quick set-up from sector templates
  • Excel and CSV import with saved column mappings
  • An alert bell for what needs your attention
  • Named roles: admin, owner, contributor, approver, viewer

How it fits the suite

One of the Pulsus Intelligence Suite.

Pulsus Accord keeps your service levels and feeds the same enterprise view as every other Pulsus app.

Feeds Pulsus Platform

Pulsus Accord publishes a summary to Pulsus Platform: attainment, coverage, critical and recurring shortfalls, and the trend.

The same bands and colours

Green, amber and red read the same way in every Pulsus app, and each company can set its own colour bands.

One Pulsus account

People sign in with their Pulsus account. Your company's plan, users and billing live on Pulsus Platform, alongside the other apps you run.

Security & privacy

Controls you can explain to an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus Accord works today.

Protected sign-in

Sign-in uses your Pulsus account or Microsoft, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check, with two-step verification when your account has it.

Your company's data, kept apart

Every table carries its company, with row-level security enabled and forced, so one company's rows never reach another.

Maker–checker on every result

A result is entered by one person and approved by another. The server refuses anyone approving their own.

Tamper-evident history

The change history is append-only and hash-chained, so an altered, removed or reordered entry shows.

Backups when your data changes

A company's data is backed up after it changes, at most every 15 minutes, with a daily sweep for anything still pending.

Writes only from this site

Every change must come from Pulsus Accord's own pages; a cross-site request is refused.
