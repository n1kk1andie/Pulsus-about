Pulsus Accord keeps the service levels your departments promise, to each other, to customers and from suppliers, written the way staff talk. Each month the owners enter what happened, someone else approves it, and Accord shows what was kept, what broke, and what keeps breaking. For government bodies, hospitals, banks, universities and any organisation that runs on promises between teams.

How it works

Enter the month. Accord judges it.

Each standard's owner enters what happened, as met or not met, "X of Y on time", or a single figure. Accord reads it against the target in force that month, and nobody approves their own result. Try it.

The approval trail

    Nothing entered? It shows as Missing, then Submission overdue after the 10th of the next month. Never as a miss: only approved results count.
