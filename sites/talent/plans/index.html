How it works

One appraisal, start to finish.

Every review follows the same path, with a date and a name against each step. Nobody approves their own review, or one they submitted.

  1. Set goalsA measure, a target, a weight and a due date, approved by the manager.
  2. Self-assessmentThe person rates their own objectives and behaviours first, with a comment.
  3. Manager reviewThe manager rates every objective and behaviour; the weighted total is scored out of 5.00.
  4. CalibrationRatings are compared across the group. Every change needs a reason.
  5. ApprovalA second-level manager or HR approves the final rating.
  6. AcknowledgementThe person acknowledges it: receipt, not agreement. It counts in every report.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Talent do?

It runs performance appraisals for a whole organisation: Balanced Scorecard objectives and behaviours rated 1 to 5, goals, calibration and the 9-box, 360 feedback, development plans and succession, with reports for each person and for the group.

Can we use our own objectives, behaviours and review steps?

Yes. Start from a neutral framework and change every word, or add conduct and risk sections where your sector needs them. You choose the review steps, the due dates, and whether a second-level manager or HR approves.

Does it fit our sector?

Pulsus Talent is for any employer, public or private: government, healthcare, banks and financial services, higher education and any organisation with many companies, departments or sites.

How does calibration work?

Calibrators compare proposed ratings across a group against a guide distribution that you set. Every change needs a reason, nobody calibrates their own rating, and the session keeps who changed what, and why. A session can follow the guide strictly or treat it as guidance.

Is 360 feedback really anonymous?

Peers, direct reports and others answer anonymously. A group's results appear only when at least three people in it have answered; smaller groups are pooled when together they reach three, or stay hidden. Nobody, not the person, their manager or HR, sees one rater's answers.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation's records are kept separate and encrypted. Every change to a rating goes into a tamper-evident history. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft, and only counts and percentages are shared with Pulsus Platform.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Talent is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Goals link to Pulsus Panorama objectives, and review progress, rating distribution, goals on track and succession cover feed your organisation's Operational Health on Pulsus Platform. It also works on its own.
