01 · Self-assessment

The person goes first.

Each person rates their own objectives and behaviours, with a comment, before their manager starts. The ratings are 1 to 5, against the targets and weights agreed for the period.

02 · Manager review

Rated against the standard.

The manager rates every objective and behaviour. The weighted total is scored out of 5.00 against the performance standard of 3.50, from Distinguished to Unsatisfactory.

03 · Calibration and approval

Checked across the group.

While a group is calibrated, approval waits. Then a second-level manager or HR approves. Nobody approves their own review, or one they submitted.

04 · Acknowledgement

Signed, and counted.

The person acknowledges their appraisal, with an optional comment. Acknowledgement confirms receipt, not agreement. The result counts in the team's Pulse and its PDF and Excel reports.

Calibration

Fair ratings. Reasons on file.

Before ratings are final, calibrators compare them across a group and against a guide distribution. Every change needs a reason, nobody calibrates their own rating, and the record keeps who changed what, and why. Try it: move someone up or down a band.
