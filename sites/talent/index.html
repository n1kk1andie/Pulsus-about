What it does

From objectives to succession.

The menu: Pulse, Team, Talent, Scorecard, History, Report and Settings.

Pulse

The team's score

The appraisal score for a unit, or the whole group rolled up.

  • Team score against the 3.50 standard
  • Rating distribution, Distinguished to Unsatisfactory
  • Averages by scorecard perspective
  • League table by department and company

Team

The appraisal itself

Each direct report's appraisal, on the organisation's own scorecard.

  • Objectives rated 1 to 5 and weighted
  • Cultural behaviours alongside the objectives
  • The reviewer's comments
  • Standard objective templates and rating scales by role

Talent

Goals, talent review and succession

Where appraisal turns into decisions about people.

  • Personal goals with a measure, target and weight, linked to Panorama objectives
  • Calibration sessions and the 9-box talent review
  • Succession plans and successor suggestions
  • 1:1s, improvement plans, pulse surveys and merit planning

Feedback

360° feedback

HR or a manager asks for feedback; raters answer once, signed in or by a one-time link.

  • The person can suggest raters; the manager approves each one
  • Raw responses are never shown to anyone
  • Averages and comments only for groups of three or more
  • Can feed the behaviour scores, by a share HR sets

Scorecard & history

Cycles that build up

Each review is a cycle; the store keeps every one.

  • Heat map of every objective across managers
  • One sheet to enter a cycle's figures
  • New cycles carry cultural ratings forward
  • Trend and heat map across cycles

Report & settings

Documents and the organisation

Formatted documents, and the organisation set up your way.

  • Team summary and individual appraisal as PDF, Excel or CSV
  • Business units grouped by company, with a group roll-up
  • Import people from a spreadsheet
  • Privacy & data: exports, leaver archiving, erasure

Security & privacy

Controls you can explain to an auditor.

Each one describes how Pulsus Talent works today.

Protected sign-in

Sign-in uses your Pulsus account, behind a Cloudflare Turnstile check, with two-step verification when your account has it.

Encrypted, with keys that rotate

Every record is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under its company's own data key. Keys can be rotated without touching the data.

People see only what they should

The server decides what each person may see: a manager sees their own people, never their own placement.

Every rating change recorded

Calibration adjustments carry a reason, and every change is kept in the rating history.

Backups when your data changes

A company's data is backed up after it changes, encrypted, with a weekly restore test.

Your people's rights

Export a person's or the company's data, archive leavers, and erase a person on request, recoverable for 30 days.
