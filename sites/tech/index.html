Why it matters

Fragmented oversight is expensive.

When technology risk lives in spreadsheets, vendor emails and separate monitoring tools, nobody sees the whole picture until something breaks.

01

Hidden exposure

Systems past end of life or end of support keep running unnoticed, carrying security and resilience risk that nobody has signed off.

02

Outages

Incidents surface through customers and branches first, and the same failures recur because no one sees the pattern behind them.

03

Wasted spend

Licences, contracts and renewals for technology no one tracks keep rolling over, and duplicate tools go unchallenged.

04

Unmanaged third-party risk

Critical services rest on providers, and their providers, with no view of concentration, materiality or contract gaps.

Technology risk is now a balance-sheet risk.
