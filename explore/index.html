How the numbers are made

Fixed rules. The same answer every time.

Operational Health and Organisational Health are calculated using fixed rules on our servers. The same inputs always produce the same results, making every figure consistent, verifiable and reproducible.

0100

Every score is shown as a number out of 100, coloured on one smooth scale from red at 0 to green at 100. Explore does not sort places into bands or categories.

Places with fewer than 20 organisations are shown but not ranked, so a handful of filers can't top a list. These are Pulsus readings — not official ratings.
