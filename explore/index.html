How the numbers are made

Fixed rules. The same answer every time.

Operational Health and Organisational Health are calculated using fixed rules on our servers. The same inputs always produce the same results, making every figure consistent, verifiable and reproducible.

Stability Index · sectors and places, from public filings
  • Stable · 66 and above
  • Steady · 45 to 65
  • Fragile · below 45
Operational Health · your organisation, from your own systems
  • Healthy · 82 and above
  • Watch · 65 to 81
  • Critical · below 65

Places with fewer than 20 organisations are shown but not ranked, so a handful of filers can't top a list. These are our own readings of public data — not official ratings.
