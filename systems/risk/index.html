Why Pulsus Risk

Your risk picture shouldn’t take a month to assemble.

When indicators live in one spreadsheet, findings in another and losses in a third, the board sees last quarter’s risk, not this month’s.

What’s at stake

When a limit is breached, someone will ask who knew.

Good risk management is not only knowing the answer. It is being able to show, at any moment, that you knew, that someone owned it, and what was done.

  • Examiners and supervisors

    They expect a current view of key risks and a clear record of every finding: who owns it, when it is due, and whether it closed.

  • The board and audit committee

    Directors need the same picture every month, judged the same way, not a new deck with new definitions each time.

  • Losses and recoveries

    Every month an indicator sits past its limit is a month of exposure. Seeing it early is cheaper than explaining it later.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Risk do?

It keeps your operational risk and audit register in one place: key risk indicators judged against your own target, tolerance and limit each month; audit findings with their rating, owner, due date and status; governance measures; and potential and fraud losses. Heat-maps and reports show how each has moved over time.

Who uses it?

The chief risk officer and risk team maintain it. Internal audit follows findings to closure. Executives, the board and the audit committee read the position each month. Updating the register is limited to administrators.

Is our data secure?

Your workbook is processed on the server, never in the browser. Uploads and edits require an administrator sign-in, sessions are signed and checked on the server, and each organisation’s data is kept separate from every other’s.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Risk is a cloud service, and it is also built to run on Microsoft Azure for institutions that need it. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

How does our data get in?

Upload the Excel workbook with your indicators, findings, governance counts and losses, and Pulsus Risk checks it and builds the register. After that, update it each month by workbook or straight in the app. Direct connections to other systems are coming.

How long does it take to set up?

If you already keep a risk workbook, you can be looking at your register the day you upload it. If not, add your indicators and their thresholds in the app, then your open findings. There is no implementation project.

Will it satisfy our regulators?

It gives examiners what they usually ask for: a current view of your key risks against appetite, and a record of every finding with its owner, dates and status. Pulsus Risk supports your compliance work; it does not certify compliance with any regulation.

Does it cover controls, compliance obligations and incidents?

Not yet. Today Pulsus Risk covers key risk indicators, audit findings, governance measures and losses. A full risk register with controls, one findings register for internal audit, external audit, regulator and compliance points, compliance obligations and incidents is in development.

Is there a separate audit product?

No. Audit findings live inside Pulsus Risk, beside the indicators and losses they relate to, so risk and audit read from one register. The findings register that is coming will extend this to external audit, regulator and compliance points, all within Pulsus Risk.

How much does it cost?

Pulsus Risk is priced for your institution, based on its size and who will use it. Talk to us about pricing, or start a free trial first.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The live demo is open to anyone, with no sign-up. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own data.
