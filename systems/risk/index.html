Why Pulsus Risk

Your risk picture shouldn’t take a month to assemble.

When risks live in one spreadsheet, findings in another and indicators in a third, the committee sees last quarter’s risk, not this month’s.

01

Risks rated once a year

Ratings are set at the workshop and not looked at again until the next one.

02

Findings that drift

Audit and regulatory points pass their due date quietly, and the first reminder is the next audit.

03

Closed on someone’s word

An owner says it is done, and nobody checks the evidence before it comes off the list.

04

Indicators judged by hand

Whether a value is “within tolerance” depends on who did the sums, and when.

What’s at stake

When a limit is breached, someone will ask who knew.

Good risk management is not only knowing the answer. It is being able to show, at any moment, that you knew, that someone owned it, and what was done.

  • Auditors, inspectors and regulators

    They expect a current view of key risks and a clear record of every finding: who owns it, when it is due, the evidence, and who checked it.

  • The board and its committees

    Members need the same picture every time, judged the same way, not a new deck with new definitions each meeting.

  • Time and exposure

    Every month a finding sits open past its date is a month of exposure. Seeing it early is cheaper than explaining it later.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Risk do?

It keeps one register for your enterprise risks, findings and key risk indicators. Risks are rated inherent, residual and target against your appetite. Findings from internal audit, external audit, regulators, compliance assessments and your own teams are followed to a validated close. Indicators are judged against their own target, tolerance and limit. A controls library links to the risks, and a committee pack comes out as PDF and Excel.

Does it fit our sector?

Yes. Pulsus Risk is used by banks and credit unions, and suits government agencies, hospitals and health systems, universities and any organisation with many sites. Your categories, rating scales and units are yours to set, and the Basel event types are there if you want them. Starter packs for each sector are coming with the next release.

Who uses it?

Named people with roles: risk admin, risk officer, auditor, owner, contributor, viewer and committee viewer. Access can be limited to the branches, sites or departments a person covers. Owners answer their findings from a phone; the committee reads the position and the pack.

How does follow-up work?

A finding is raised with an owner and a due date. The owner gives a management response and an action plan with dates, then adds evidence, including photos from their phone, and submits. The person who raised it validates and closes it, or returns it. Findings can be reopened or withdrawn. Reminders go before the due date, on it and once it is overdue, then escalate to the owner’s manager and the executive sponsor. They appear in the app today; email is coming.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation’s data is kept separate from every other’s. Evidence files are stored privately, with a fingerprint recorded when each is added. Every change goes into a tamper-evident history. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Risk is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

How do we get our register in?

Add your risks, findings and indicators in the app. If you used the earlier Pulsus Risk workbook, it is imported once when you move across. Excel and CSV import templates with column mapping are coming; until then, talk to us about bringing an existing register in.

Will it satisfy our auditors and regulators?

It gives them what they usually ask for: a current view of your key risks against appetite, and a record of every finding with its owner, dates, evidence and who validated it. Pulsus Risk supports your compliance work; it does not certify compliance with any regulation.

Does it cover control testing, obligations and incidents?

Not yet. The controls library is live and linked to risks. Control testing and RCSA, a compliance obligations register, and incidents and loss events are in development. Your existing potential and fraud loss history is kept.

How much does it cost?

$350 a month, including 5 users; extra users are $40 each a month. Pulsus Risk and Pulsus Quality together, as Pulsus Assurance, are $500 a month. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card. For a larger organisation, talk to us.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The live demo is open to anyone, with no sign-up. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own data.
