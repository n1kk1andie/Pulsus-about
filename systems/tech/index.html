Application & service intelligence

Track availability, SLA performance, criticality and service trends across the application portfolio.

Network & incident intelligence

Monitor network performance, service disruptions, SLA breaches and incident command history.

Technology lifecycle

See EOL and EOS exposure before ageing technology becomes an operational, security or resilience risk.

Outsourcing & third-party risk

Maintain the outsourcing register and assess materiality, criticality, approvals, renewals, contractual gaps and provider concentration.

Dependency intelligence

Understand how applications, infrastructure, services, vendors and fourth parties connect — exposing concentrations and single points of failure.

Resilience & data

Track continuity, recovery, data protection and supporting evidence against critical technology and third-party arrangements.
