Why Pulsus Quality

What you don’t see becomes your risk.

Small defects, repeat mistakes and missed standards add up quietly: lost time, rework, higher costs and customers who notice before you do.

What’s at stake

Quality isn’t a department. It’s a discipline.

Every file a branch processes is a promise to a customer and a record an examiner may one day read. Consistency across branches is what keeps both.

  • Customer confidence

    A name misspelled, a form unsigned or a transfer delayed is felt by a member long before it shows in a report.

  • Audit and examination

    Auditors and regulators expect to see that issues were found, fixed and checked, with a record of who did what.

  • Time and cost

    Rework, follow-up calls and repeated corrections consume the hours your branches should spend serving members.

See it in action

Finding the issue is only the beginning.

Every review follows the same path, from the branch file to a verified fix. Nothing counts until a supervisor approves it, and nothing closes until someone else has checked it.

  1. Review

    A reviewer samples files in a branch and checks each against your checklist.

  2. Submit

    The completed review goes to a supervisor, with any supporting evidence attached.

  3. Approve & publish

    Once approved, the results flow into every dashboard and report at once.

  4. Raise an action

    A supervisor raises a corrective action with an owner, priority and due date.

  5. Fix & evidence

    The branch records what it did and attaches the proof.

  6. Verify & close

    A different supervisor verifies the fix, or sends it back with a note.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Quality do?

It brings your branch quality reviews into one place. You see the defect, resolution and recurring rates for every branch and process area, a scored operational standard with audit grades, and a register of corrective actions followed through to a verified close.

Who uses it?

Quality reviewers key reviews and see their branch’s actions. Supervisors approve reviews, raise actions and verify fixes. Branch and operations leaders compare branches. Executives and the board read the trend and the report. Each role sees what it is allowed to see; officers see only their own branch’s actions.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation’s data is kept in its own private storage area, and no request made for one organisation can read another’s. Files are stored privately, sign-in sessions are signed and checked on the server, and passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Quality is a cloud service, with each organisation’s data held in its own private storage area. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

How does our data get in?

Two ways today. Reviewers can key reviews in the app, or complete a review template in Excel and upload it. You can also upload the defects or operational standard workbook you already keep, and Pulsus builds the history from it. Direct connections to other systems are coming.

What is coming next?

Mobile inspections on a phone or tablet with photo evidence, review scheduling across the network, and branch league tables. They are in development and not yet available; the live demo shows the product as it is today.

How long does it take to set up?

There is no implementation project. An administrator sets up your branches, process areas, checklists and severities in the app, starting from a sample checklist if you like, or uploads your workbook. Most of the work is deciding what you want to check.

Will it satisfy our regulators and auditors?

It gives them what they usually ask for: evidence that issues were found, owned, fixed and independently checked, with a history of every step. Pulsus Quality supports your compliance work; it does not certify compliance with any regulation.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Pulsus Quality feeds Pulsus Platform’s Quality and Compliance drivers, so its readings appear in your organisation’s Operational Health beside the other applications you run. It also works on its own.

How much does it cost?

Pulsus Quality is priced for your institution, based on the size of your network and who will use it. Talk to us about pricing, or start a free trial first.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The live demo is open to anyone, with no sign-up. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own branches and data.
