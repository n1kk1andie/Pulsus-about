Why Pulsus Quality

What you don’t see becomes your risk.

Small defects, repeat mistakes and missed standards add up quietly: lost time, rework, higher costs and customers, patients or students who notice before you do.

01

Findings stay in files

Review results live in spreadsheets and emails, so nobody sees every site at once.

02

03

04

Monthly packs arrive late and in different shapes, so the board hears anecdotes, not a trend.

What’s at stake

Quality isn’t a department. It’s a discipline.

Every file, visit or transaction a site handles is a promise to someone, and a record an auditor or inspector may one day read. Consistency across sites is what keeps both.

  • The people you serve

    A name misspelled, a form unsigned or a request delayed is felt by a customer, patient or student long before it shows in a report.

  • Audit and inspection

    Auditors, inspectors and regulators expect to see that issues were found, fixed and checked, with a record of who did what.

  • Time and cost

    Rework, follow-up calls and repeated corrections consume the hours your sites should spend on their real work.

See it in action

Finding the issue is only the beginning.

Every review follows the same path, from the site to a verified fix. Nothing counts until someone else approves it, and nothing closes until someone else has checked it.

  1. Review

    An inspector checks a sample on a phone. It is saved on the device as they type.

  2. Submit

    The finished review goes to the approval queue.

  3. Approve & publish

    Approved by someone other than its author, it flows into every dashboard.

  4. Raise an action

    With a root cause, an owner, a due date and, if you like, a named verifier.

  5. Fix & evidence

    The site records what it did and attaches evidence and photos.

  6. Verify & close

    Someone who didn’t raise, submit or own it verifies the fix, or sends it back.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Pulsus Quality do?

It brings your quality reviews into one place. Inspectors carry out reviews on a phone; supervisors approve them. You see the defect, resolution and recurring rates for every site and process area, an Operational Standard scorecard you set up yourself, and a register of corrective and preventive actions followed through to a verified close.

Does it fit our sector?

Yes. Pulsus Quality is used by banks and credit unions, and suits government agencies, hospitals and health systems, universities and any organisation with many sites. Your sites, process areas, checks and scorecard are yours to set. Starter packs for each sector are coming with the next release.

Who uses it?

Named people with roles: admin, supervisor, inspector, branch manager and viewer. Access can be limited to some sites. Inspectors carry out reviews, supervisors approve them and verify fixes, site managers follow their own actions, and executives read the trend and the report.

Can we review on a phone?

Yes. A review is saved on the device as you type, then submitted to the approval queue. Nobody approves their own review. A full offline app, for sites with no signal, is coming.

Is our data secure?

Each organisation’s data is kept separate from every other’s. Every review and action goes into a tamper-evident history. Staff sign in with a Pulsus account or with Microsoft.

We are happy to take your information-security team through the detail.

Where is our data stored?

Pulsus Quality is a cloud service. If your regulator or policy requires data to stay in a particular country or region, talk to us before you start and we will confirm where your data will be held.

What is coming next?

Inspection programmes and scheduling, league tables, signature capture, an offline app, an API and webhooks, sending serious defects to Pulsus Risk, and sector starter packs. They are in development and not yet available; the live demo shows the product as it is today.

Will it satisfy our auditors and inspectors?

It gives them what they usually ask for: evidence that issues were found, owned, fixed and independently checked, with a history of every step. Pulsus Quality supports your compliance work; it does not certify compliance with any regulation.

Does it work with the rest of Pulsus?

Yes. Pulsus Quality feeds Pulsus Platform’s Quality and Compliance drivers, so its readings appear in your organisation’s Operational Health beside the other applications you run. It also works on its own.

How much does it cost?

$250 a month, including 5 users; extra users are $30 each a month. Pulsus Quality and Pulsus Risk together, as Pulsus Assurance, are $500 a month. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no card. For a larger organisation, talk to us.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The live demo is open to anyone, with no sign-up. When you are ready, start a free trial with your own sites and data.
