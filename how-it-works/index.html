3Executive Intelligence

For leadership and the board: how healthy the organisation is, why, and what will bring it back.

Pulse

Operational Health, out of 100.

One number, its band, its trend and its outlook — with Organisational Health alongside.

Diagnosis

The cause chain, step by step.

Which readings turned worse first, how they spread, and exactly how many points each takes off. Links stay "suspected" until someone confirms them with evidence.

Treatment

Suggested actions for each cause, with owners and dates.

Recovery

How fast you're getting back to healthy, and what's still holding you back.

Calibrate

How you compare with peers in your sector and place.

Plus a Board Pack — a point-in-time report you can print or save as PDF.

How the numbers are made

Fixed rules. The same answer every time.

Operational Health and Organisational Health are calculated using fixed rules on our servers. The same inputs always produce the same results, making every figure consistent, verifiable and reproducible.

Stability Index · sectors and places, from public data
  • Stable · 66 and above
  • Steady · 45 to 65
  • Fragile · below 45
Operational Health · your organisation, from your own systems
  • Healthy · 82 and above
  • Watch · 65 to 81
  • Critical · below 65

Places with fewer than 20 organisations are shown but not ranked, so a handful of filers can't top a list. These are our own readings of public data — not official ratings.
